## Step prompts

### 01 — Your floor plan
Top-down architectural floor plan of a simple rectangular house. Black outline walls, dashed centre lines, door arc on one wall, dimension arrows on two sides. Yellow dot at each corner. White background.

### 02 — Level the ground
Cross-section view. Horizontal ground line with diagonal hatching below. Spirit level tool resting on the ground surface. Yellow bubble centred in the level vial. Minimal.

### 03 — Mark the corners
Top-down view. Dashed rectangle outline (string lines). Yellow dot stake marker at each corner with a small vertical stake drawn below each dot.

### 04 — Pour the footings
Cross-section. Narrow trench dug into hatched soil. Rectangular concrete strip filling the trench with cross-hatch fill pattern. Yellow line along the top surface of the concrete.

### 05 — Install floor U-blocks
Top-down view. Perimeter rectangle of yellow U-channel sections laid end-to-end, forming a continuous frame. Interior is blank white. Gaps between sections show individual block segments.

### 06 — Connect corner channels
Close-up top-down detail. Two U-channel sections meeting at a 90° corner. Yellow highlighted corner joint. Two small black rivet dots near the joint.

### 07 — The corrugated panel
Side profile view. Single corrugated sheet shown as a clean sinusoidal wave. Yellow dots at each wave crest. Dimension arrows: width 0.81 m horizontal, height vertical on left side.

### 08 — Insert the first panel
Elevation view. Tall rectangular corrugated panel (vertical wave lines inside) sliding downward into a yellow U-channel at the base. Black arrow pointing downward above the panel.

### 09 — Rivet to the channel
Close-up cross-section. Panel foot inside U-channel. Rivet tool above, black rivet shaft passing through panel and channel flange. Yellow circle highlighting the rivet set point at bottom.

### 10 — Overlap the next panel
Top-down view. Two rectangular panels side by side. Yellow shaded overlap zone (~10 cm) where they interlock. Dimension arrows showing overlap width.

### 11 — Rivet the overlap
Elevation view. Two overlapping panels. Three black rivet dots along the overlap seam (top, middle, bottom). Yellow circle highlight around the middle rivet.

### 12 — Continue around the perimeter
Top-down view. All four walls filled with alternating panel sections (alternating white/light-grey fills). Small black rivet dots along each wall. Yellow panel count badge in the centre of the floor area showing a number.

### 13 — Cap with top U-block
Elevation view. Full-width wall with panel wave lines. Yellow U-channel hovering above the panel tops with three downward arrows showing it sliding into place. Bottom U-channel also visible.

### 14 — Install roof purlins
Simple isometric view. Two rectangular walls (front and right face). Three black timber/steel beams spanning from wall to wall across the top. Yellow top U-channel visible along wall tops.

### 15 — Lay the roof sheets
Elevation / front view. Symmetrical pitched roof. Corrugated sheets shown as diagonal lines on both slopes. Yellow ridge highlight at apex. Wall below is a plain rectangle.

### 16 — Rivet the roof
Close-up elevation. Corrugated roof sheet wave visible. Rivet gun tool held over a crest. Black rivet shaft. Yellow circle where rivet meets purlin below.

### 17 — Frame the door & window
Wall elevation. Full rectangular wall with corrugated wave lines. Rectangle door opening cut out (white inside). Yellow sub-frame border around the door opening. Black rivet dots along frame edges.

### 18 — Move in
Simple clean house front elevation. Rectangular walls, symmetrical pitched roof with corrugated lines. Two windows (yellow-tinted glazing). Centre door. Yellow circle with black checkmark in top-right corner.
